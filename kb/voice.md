# Voice (from the Transline content pack and the website; docs/research/03 §1.4)

- Open on a concrete, checkable observation about THEM, never on us.
- Specific, verifiable numbers only. Specificity is the single biggest difference between approved and rejected drafts.
- Operator-to-operator register: capital already spent, pilot vs running, shelf space, landed cost.
- One quotable line per piece at most.
- Flag any unsourced claim explicitly rather than soften it into vagueness.
- Plain English. No hype, no flattery, no exclamation marks, no emojis.
- Do NOT use the website's "we hate traditional consulting" or "stop wasting money" lines in cold outreach. They read as aggressive to a CFO.
- Email first touch: under 120 words, one ask (a 20-minute call OR the free 2-page pulse), one signal, one insight.
- LinkedIn note: under 300 characters, a reason to connect grounded in one fact, no pitch, no price.
- Always end an email with the opt-out line from compliance.md.
