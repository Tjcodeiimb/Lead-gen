# Methodology

Diagnose → Plan → Execute. Shipped by week 4 where a traditional firm is still scoping at week 12.
Research is AI-accelerated and human-verified: every number carries a source, a date and a label of
known / estimated / undisclosed. We never estimate what isn't disclosed. Primary research (expert and
consumer interviews, surveys) is done by people. Judgement, the "so what" and the final model logic are human.
Three service lines: (1) market-entry strategy, (2) consumer-psychology and business research,
(3) operations and finance modelling. Corridors: India↔UK, India↔UAE/GCC, US↔India, EU↔India (2027).
Partners: Scoutline Capital (fundraising, financial modelling), Brown Men Marketing (growth marketing).
